# MementoCare AI — Smart Reminders & Routine Support

## Tagline
**"AI that remembers the person, not just the score."**

---

## 1. Routine Support Philosophy

Establishing predictable daily routines is essential for individuals with memory loss. MementoCare AI provides a non-intrusive, voice-assisted reminder system that helps seniors stay on track with daily activities, hydration, nutrition, and scheduled care.

---

## 2. Supported Reminder Categories

| Category | Example Event | Audio / Visual Prompt |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Medicine Schedule** | Morning Tablet (Scheduled by Caregiver) | "Good morning Abeni. Your reminder says it is time for your morning medicine." |
| **Hydration** | Drink Water | "A refreshing glass of water is ready for you." |
| **Meals & Nutrition** | Afternoon Lunch | "Time for a warm lunch." |
| **Cognitive Activities** | Daily Memory Game | "Would you like to explore your family memory activity today?" |
| **Family Connection** | Evening Call with Daughter | "Priyanka will call at 6:00 PM." |
| **Circadian Routine** | Evening Rest / Bedtime | "Time to rest and listen to peaceful river sounds." |

---

## 3. Strict Medication Safety Boundary

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **MementoCare AI DOES NOT PRESCRIBE MEDICATIONS OR ADVISE ON DOSAGES.**
> 
> - All medication schedules are entered directly by the **authorized caregiver or clinician**.
> - The application announces schedules: *"Your reminder says it is time for your medicine."*
> - The application **NEVER** calculates doses, modifies prescriptions, or suggests pharmacological treatments.

---

## 4. Reminder Lifecycle & Escalation

```text
Scheduled Time → Gentle Audio Chime + Spoken Prompt → Big 'Acknowledged' Button
                                                     ↓
                                         If Not Acknowledged in 45m
                                                     ↓
                                       Caregiver Daily Reassurance Flag
                                    ("Reminder not acknowledged. Consider checking in.")
```

- Reminders never emit shrill or alarming sirens that could trigger agitation.
- Caregivers receive non-alarmist status updates to maintain peace of mind.
