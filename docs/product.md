# MementoCare AI — Product Specification & Philosophy

## Product Identity
- **Product Name:** MementoCare AI
- **SIH Problem Statement:** SIH26003 — AI-Based Cognitive Gaming and Memory Assistance Platform for Elderly Dementia Patients in the North Eastern Region
- **Organisation:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)
- **Tagline:** *"AI that remembers the person, not just the score."*
- **Positioning:** *"Personal. Adaptive. Voice-enabled. Offline. Connected to care."*

---

## 1. Core Philosophy & Value Proposition

MementoCare AI is not merely a collection of generic brain games; it is an integrated **cognitive engagement and memory-support ecosystem** designed around the human experience of elderly seniors and their care circles in North East India.

```text
PERSON
  ↓
PERSONAL MEMORY (People, Places, Events, Objects, Preferences, Routine)
  ↓
COGNITIVE ACTIVITY (5 MVP Games)
  ↓
VOICE / TOUCH (STT / TTS with Tap & Type Fallback)
  ↓
ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY (Accuracy 45% + Speed 25% + Consistency 20% + Assistance 10%)
  ↓
OFFLINE CONTINUITY (100% playable in monsoonal blackouts)
  ↓
SAFE SYNCHRONISATION (Idempotent event outbox)
  ↓
CAREGIVER (Memory approvals, reassurance, daily routines)
  ↓
ASHA / HEALTH WORKER (Cluster triage, lightweight village visits)
  ↓
AUTHORISED CLINICIAN (Non-diagnostic longitudinal trend review)
```

---

## 2. Medical Safety Boundary & Claim Policy

> **Statutory Disclaimer:** *"MementoCare AI supports cognitive engagement, routine assistance, and caregiver visibility. It does not diagnose dementia, measure disease severity, prescribe medicines, change medication, or replace doctors or qualified healthcare workers."*

### Terminology Rules
- Prohibited: *"Dementia score"*, *"Disease progression"*, *"Alzheimer's detected"*, *"Cure/Prevent dementia"*.
- Mandated: *"Activity performance"*, *"Engagement trend"*, *"Review prompt"*, *"Assistance used"*, *"Activity consistency"*.
