# MementoCare AI — ASHA & Community Health Worker Portal

## 1. Role in the North Eastern Health Ecosystem

Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs) and community health workers act as the vital bridge connecting remote village households in North East India with the health system.

---

## 2. Speed-Optimized Village Visit Workflow

- **Cluster Overview:** Displays daily triage priorities (`🔴 Check-in recommended`, `🟡 Follow-up`, `🟢 Routine`).
- **Triage Rationale:** Transparent workflow reasons (e.g. *"2 missed hydration reminders; caregiver requested visit"* or *"Device offline 48h; sync pending"*) rather than automated medical severity scores.
- **Fast 10-Second Visit Logging:** Allows the ASHA to select a senior, review daily adherence, record qualitative field observations, and stage sync events.
- **Offline Village Sync:** Staged visits synchronize automatically when network connectivity returns at the primary health centre (PHC).
- **Escalation Path:** One-tap referral communication with Dr. Ananya Sharma (GMCH).
