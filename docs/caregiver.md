# MementoCare AI — Caregiver Portal & Workflow

## 1. Objective: Reassurance & Simplicity

Caregivers of elderly seniors with memory decline experience high emotional burden. The caregiver portal prioritizes **simplicity, reassurance, and actionable visibility** rather than overwhelming clinical telemetry.

---

## 2. Core Capabilities

1. **Personal Memory Manager:**
   - Upload family photographs across 6 pillars (`People`, `Places`, `Events`, `Objects`, `Preferences`, `Routine`).
   - Assign human labels and approve AI-generated bounded activity drafts.
2. **Routine & Reminders:**
   - Configure hydration, meals, appointments, and daily family check-in calls.
   - Acknowledge routine updates without clinical medication prescription claims.
3. **Longitudinal Engagement Trends:**
   - 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day charts for Activity Completion, Interaction Score, Assistance Used, Response Latency, and Difficulty Progression.
4. **Caregiver Observation Journal:**
   - Record daily qualitative notes (Mood, Sleep, Appetite, Clarity) shared directly with authorized healthcare professionals.
