# MementoCare AI — Music & Reminiscence Audio Module

## Tagline
**"AI that remembers the person, not just the score."**

---

## 1. Module Overview

Music and acoustic cues have a well-documented ability to evoke emotional familiarity, grounding, and calm in seniors living with cognitive decline. In the North Eastern Region, traditional acoustic instruments and natural soundscapes (monsoon rain, Brahmaputra river streams, birdsong) carry deep personal resonance.

---

## 2. Audio Categories & Regional Instruments

| Category | Instruments / Soundscapes | Cultural Origin | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traditional Acoustic** | Pepa (Buffalo Horn), Dhol, Gogona, Bahi (Bamboo Flute) | Assam (Bihu) | Joyful cultural recollection, active engagement |
| **Hills & Flutes** | Khasi Tangmuri, Bamboo Flute | Meghalaya | Serene contemplation, relaxation |
| **Harvest Rhythms** | Cheraw Bamboo Beats, Mizo Drum | Mizoram | Pattern recognition, rhythmic timing |
| **Classical & Folk** | Pena, Manipuri Pung | Manipur | Circadian rhythm alignment, evening calm |
| **Nature Soundscapes** | Morning River, Monsoon Rainfall, Tea Garden Breezes, Hill Forest Birds | North East Landscapes | Grounding, sensory comfort, sleep hygiene |

---

## 3. Playback Controls & Senior Accessibility

The Music & Memory player is engineered with elderly-centric UI:
- **Oversized Controls:** Minimum 64px circular Play/Pause, Next, Previous, and Favorite buttons.
- **Visual Feedback:** Waveform and river visualization providing serene sensory feedback without sensory overload.
- **Repeat & Loop:** One-touch toggle to repeat calming tracks continuously.
- **Volume Limiter:** Built-in dynamic compression preventing jarring acoustic spikes.
