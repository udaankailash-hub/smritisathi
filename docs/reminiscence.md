---

## 1. Cultural Reminiscence Philosophy

Reminiscence therapy uses tangible prompts—such as photographs, everyday domestic artifacts, traditional textiles, and regional stories—to help seniors recall long-term memories, sparking positive social interaction and family storytelling.

MementoCare AI integrates two complementary reminiscence streams:
1. **Personal Reminiscence:** Caregiver-uploaded family photographs, familiar domestic spaces, and personal milestones.
2. **NER Cultural Heritage Gallery:** Curated regional imagery celebrating the living traditions, crafts, agriculture, and festivals of North East India.

## 3. Human Review & Content Governance

Every cultural reminiscence item in MementoCare AI follows a strict governance pipeline:
```text
Draft Submission → Regional Reviewer Validation → Cultural Accuracy Check → Published to Catalog
```
- Content is tagged by state, language, and cultural significance.
- No copyrighted or unverified media is added without proper licensing or public-domain verification.
- Content is presented with dignity, avoiding stereotypes or historical inaccuracies.
