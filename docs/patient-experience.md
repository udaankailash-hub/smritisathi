# MementoCare AI — Elderly Patient Experience Specification

## 1. UX Principles for Seniors

The senior interface is intentionally minimalist, calming, and free of clutter:
- **Low Navigation Depth:** Exactly 4 main tabs (`Today`, `Activities`, `Memories`, `Help`).
- **Large Touch Targets:** Minimum 64px–72px height for all interactive cards and buttons.
- **High Contrast & Large Typography:** Legible 18px–28px font size with soothing dark teal/slate backgrounds (`#07111F`, `#101F31`, `#19C3B1`).
- **One Primary Action:** Prominent *"Start Today's Activity"* hero button.
- **Always Available Controls:** Every game includes `Repeat Instruction`, `Gentle Hint`, `Help`, `Pause / Resume`, and `Exit`.
- **Zero Time Pressure:** No countdown timers that penalize or induce anxiety.
- **Comforting Audio:** Chimes and voice prompts speak at a calm 0.85x rate in native regional dialects.
