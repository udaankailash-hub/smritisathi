# MementoCare AI — Authorized Healthcare Worker & Clinician Dome

## 1. Clinician Telemetry Scope

Authorized clinicians (e.g. Dr. Ananya Sharma at Gauhati Medical College & Hospital) receive restricted, read-only summaries of patient activity engagement:
- **Longitudinal Interaction Consistency:** Session frequency and completion rates.
- **Assistance & Hint Utilization:** Non-diagnostic metric reflecting interaction comfort.
- **Caregiver & ASHA Field Observations:** Qualitative insights into mood, sleep, and appetite.
- **Non-Diagnostic Report Export:** Printable PDF and summary exports with mandatory medical safety disclaimers.

> **Clinician Boundary:** *"MementoCare AI provides non-diagnostic application interaction information only. It does not replace professional clinical assessments, neurological testing, or qualified medical diagnosis."*
